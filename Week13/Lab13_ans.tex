\section{Question 1} 

\subsection{a)}

The computation of $M$ on $w=111$ is
\[
q_0 111\bot \;\vdash\; 1q_1 11\bot \;\vdash\; 11q_0 1\bot
\;\vdash\; 111q_1\bot \;\vdash\; 111\bot q_{\text{acc}}.
\]
Thus the $5 \times 5$ tableau is as follows:

\begin{table}[htbp]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
$q_0$ & 1 & 1 & 1 & $\bot$ \\
\hline
1 & $q_1$ & 1 & 1 & $\bot$ \\
\hline
1 & 1 & $q_0$ & 1 & $\bot$ \\
\hline
1 & 1 & 1 & $q_1$ & $\bot$ \\
\hline
1 & 1 & 1 & $\bot$ & $q_{\text{acc}}$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{The $5 \times 5$ tableau for $M$ on input $111$.}
\label{tab:5x5}
\end{table}

\subsection{b)}

Let $x_{i,j,s}$ be the Boolean variable which is true iff the cell in row $i$ and
column $j$ of the tableau contains symbol/state $s$.

For the input $w=111$, the starting configuration is $q_0 111\bot$, so
\[
\phi_{\text{start}}
= x_{1,1,q_0} \land x_{1,2,1} \land x_{1,3,1}
\land x_{1,4,1} \land x_{1,5,\bot}.
\]

The accepting condition is that $q_{\text{acc}}$ appears somewhere in the tableau:
\[
\phi_{\text{accept}}
= \bigvee_{i=1}^{5}\bigvee_{j=1}^{5} x_{i,j,q_{\text{acc}}}.
\]
In this tableau, this is satisfied because $x_{5,5,q_{\text{acc}}}$ is true.
